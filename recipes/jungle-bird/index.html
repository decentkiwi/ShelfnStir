Bitter Tiki

Jungle Bird

Dark rum and pineapple pulled into focus by Campari's bitter snap.

Bitter Tiki 7 min Tropical
Batch

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 oz dark rum
  • 1 1/2 oz pineapple juice
  • 3/4 oz Campari
  • 1/2 oz lime juice
  • 1/2 oz simple syrup

Smart swaps

dark rum: Aged rum works when you want a lighter version.

lime juice: Use lemon only in flexible sours. Keep lime for Margaritas, Daiquiris, Mojitos, and tiki drinks.

simple syrup: Use honey syrup or agave syrup, but start slightly lighter because they read sweeter.

Method

  1. Shake with ice.
  2. Strain over crushed ice.
  3. Garnish with pineapple leaves if available.
