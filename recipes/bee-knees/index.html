Honey Gin Sour

Bee's Knees

Gin, lemon, and honey syrup. Floral, clean, and very pantry-friendly.

Honey Gin Sour 5 min Golden
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 oz gin
  • 3/4 oz lemon juice
  • 3/4 oz honey syrup

Smart swaps

lemon juice: Use lime for a sharper version in many sours, but expect the drink to taste brighter.

honey syrup: Use simple syrup for a cleaner drink or agave syrup for a softer roundness.

Method

  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.
  2. Strain into a coupe.
  3. Garnish with lemon.

Keep pouring
