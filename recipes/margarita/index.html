Classic Citrus

Margarita

The lime-bright tequila standard, sharpened with orange liqueur and a salt rim.

Classic Citrus 5 min Bright
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • 1 oz fresh lime juice
  • 3/4 oz orange liqueur
  • 1/2 oz agave syrup
  • Salt, optional

Smart swaps

lime juice: Use lemon only in flexible sours. Keep lime for Margaritas, Daiquiris, Mojitos, and tiki drinks.

orange liqueur: Orange curacao works in tiki drinks. Triple sec works in most citrus sours.

agave syrup: Use simple syrup in the same amount if the drink does not need extra agave flavor.

Method

  1. Shake tequila, lime, orange liqueur, and agave with ice.
  2. Strain into a salted rocks glass over fresh ice.
  3. Garnish with a lime wheel.
