Stirred Classic

Manhattan

Rye, sweet vermouth, and aromatic bitters with a cherry finish.

Stirred Classic 5 min Silky
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 oz rye whiskey
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters
  • Brandied cherry

Smart swaps

rye whiskey: Bourbon works when you want a rounder and sweeter version.

Method

  1. Stir whiskey, vermouth, and bitters with ice.
  2. Strain into a coupe.
  3. Garnish with a brandied cherry.
