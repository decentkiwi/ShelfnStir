Two-Ingredient Aperitivo

Garibaldi

Campari and fresh orange juice, aerated until soft and sunny.

Two-Ingredient Aperitivo 4 min Fluffy
Batch

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 oz Campari
  • 4 oz orange juice

Method

  1. Whip or shake orange juice to aerate.
  2. Build over ice with Campari.
  3. Stir gently.
