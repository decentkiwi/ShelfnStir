Bitter Classic

Negroni

Equal parts gin, Campari, and sweet vermouth. Austere, rosy, and endlessly riffable.

Bitter Classic 4 min Bitter
Batch

Ingredients

  • 1 oz gin
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • Orange peel

Method

  1. Stir liquid ingredients with ice until chilled.
  2. Strain over one large cube.
  3. Express orange peel and garnish.
