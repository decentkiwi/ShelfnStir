Tequila Highball

Paloma

Tequila, lime, grapefruit soda, and salt. Brisk, simple, and hard to improve.

Tequila Highball 5 min Zesty Highball
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • 1/2 oz lime juice
  • Grapefruit soda
  • Pinch of salt

Smart swaps

lime juice: Use lemon only in flexible sours. Keep lime for Margaritas, Daiquiris, Mojitos, and tiki drinks.

Method

  1. Build tequila, lime, and salt in a highball with ice.
  2. Top with grapefruit soda.
  3. Stir and garnish with grapefruit.

Serve it in

Highball

Tall and narrow keeps carbonation from escaping and packs the ice tight, so the drink stays cold and fizzy to the last sip.
