Tropical Rum

Painkiller

Dark rum, pineapple, orange, coconut, and nutmeg.

Tropical Rum 7 min Sunny
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 oz dark rum
  • 4 oz pineapple juice
  • 1 oz orange juice
  • 1 oz coconut cream
  • Nutmeg

Smart swaps

dark rum: Aged rum works when you want a lighter version.

Method

  1. Shake with ice.
  2. Pour over crushed ice.
  3. Grate nutmeg over the top.
