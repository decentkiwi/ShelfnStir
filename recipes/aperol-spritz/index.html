Low-ABV Spritz

Aperol Spritz

Aperol, prosecco, and soda water over ice with an orange slice.

Low-ABV Spritz 4 min Light
Batch

Ingredients

  • 3 oz prosecco
  • 2 oz Aperol
  • 1 oz soda water
  • Orange slice

Smart swaps

prosecco: Any dry sparkling wine works.

soda water: Sparkling water works as a direct swap.

Method

  1. Build in a wine glass over ice.
  2. Add prosecco, Aperol, and soda.
  3. Stir gently and garnish with orange.
