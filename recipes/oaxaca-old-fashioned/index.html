Agave Stirred

Oaxaca Old Fashioned

Reposado tequila and mezcal softened with agave and bitters.

Agave Stirred 5 min Smoky
Batch

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 oz reposado tequila
  • 1/2 oz mezcal
  • 1 tsp agave syrup
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters
  • Orange peel

Smart swaps

agave syrup: Use simple syrup in the same amount if the drink does not need extra agave flavor.

Method

  1. Stir everything with ice.
  2. Strain over a large cube.
  3. Garnish with a flamed orange peel if desired.

Keep pouring
