Low-ABV Highball

Americano

Campari, sweet vermouth, and soda, brightened with orange.

Low-ABV Highball 4 min Leisurely
Batch

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 oz Campari
  • 1 1/2 oz sweet vermouth
  • Soda water
  • Orange slice

Smart swaps

soda water: Sparkling water works as a direct swap.

Method

  1. Build Campari and vermouth over ice.
  2. Top with soda.
  3. Garnish with orange.
