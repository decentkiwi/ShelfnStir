Corpse Reviver No. 2

Gin, Lillet, orange liqueur, lemon, and a whisper of absinthe.

Niche Classic 6 min Bracing
Batch

Ingredients

  • 3/4 oz gin
  • 3/4 oz Lillet Blanc
  • 3/4 oz orange liqueur
  • 3/4 oz lemon juice
  • Absinthe rinse

Smart swaps

orange liqueur: Orange curacao works in tiki drinks. Triple sec works in most citrus sours.

lemon juice: Use lime for a sharper version in many sours, but expect the drink to taste brighter.

Method

  1. Rinse a chilled coupe with absinthe.
  2. Shake remaining ingredients with ice.
  3. Strain into the coupe.
