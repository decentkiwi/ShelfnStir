Whiskey Negroni

Boulevardier

Bourbon or rye in place of gin, rounded by Campari and sweet vermouth.

Whiskey Negroni 5 min Rich
Batch

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 oz bourbon
  • 1 oz Campari
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • Orange peel

Smart swaps

bourbon: Rye whiskey works when you want a drier and spicier version.

Method

  1. Stir all liquid ingredients with ice.
  2. Strain over a large cube.
  3. Garnish with orange peel.
