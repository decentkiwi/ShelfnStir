Classic Sour

Whiskey Sour

Bourbon, lemon, sugar, and optional egg white for a plush foam.

Classic Sour 6 min Velvety
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 oz bourbon
  • 3/4 oz lemon juice
  • 3/4 oz simple syrup
  • 1 egg white, optional
  • Angostura bitters

Smart swaps

bourbon: Rye whiskey works when you want a drier and spicier version.

lemon juice: Use lime for a sharper version in many sours, but expect the drink to taste brighter.

simple syrup: Use honey syrup or agave syrup, but start slightly lighter because they read sweeter.

Method

  1. Shake bourbon, lemon, syrup, and egg white without ice if using.
  2. Shake again with ice.
  3. Strain and dot the foam with bitters.
