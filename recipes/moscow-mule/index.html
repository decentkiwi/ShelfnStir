Vodka Highball

Moscow Mule

Vodka, lime, and ginger beer, served cold enough to frost the cup.

Vodka Highball 4 min Spicy Copper mug
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 oz vodka
  • 1/2 oz lime juice
  • Ginger beer
  • Mint or lime wedge

Smart swaps

lime juice: Use lemon only in flexible sours. Keep lime for Margaritas, Daiquiris, Mojitos, and tiki drinks.

Method

  1. Build vodka and lime over ice.
  2. Top with ginger beer.
  3. Stir once and garnish.

Serve it in

Copper mug

Copper conducts cold quickly, so the mug frosts over and keeps the drink icy. The handle also keeps your hand off the cold metal.
