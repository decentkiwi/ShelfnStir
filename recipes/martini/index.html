Stirred Classic

Dry Martini

Cold gin, dry vermouth, and a lemon twist or olive. Minimalism with consequences.

Stirred Classic 4 min Clean Martini glass
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 oz gin
  • 1/2 oz dry vermouth
  • Orange bitters, optional
  • Lemon twist or olive

Method

  1. Stir gin and vermouth with plenty of ice.
  2. Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.
  3. Garnish with lemon or an olive.

Serve it in

Martini glass

The wide surface lifts the aroma of a lemon twist or olive and keeps a very cold drink looking sharp. It warms fast, so pour smaller drinks and finish them quickly.
