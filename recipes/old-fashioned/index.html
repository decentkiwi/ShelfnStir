Whiskey Classic

Old Fashioned

Whiskey, bitters, sugar, and citrus oil built slowly over ice.

Whiskey Classic 5 min Spirit-forward
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 oz bourbon or rye
  • 1 tsp simple syrup
  • 2 dashes Angostura bitters
  • Orange twist

Smart swaps

simple syrup: Use honey syrup or agave syrup, but start slightly lighter because they read sweeter.

Method

  1. Combine syrup and bitters in a rocks glass.
  2. Add whiskey and ice, then stir until cold.
  3. Garnish with an orange twist.
