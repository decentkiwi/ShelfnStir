Vodka Sour

Cosmopolitan

Vodka, cranberry, lime, and orange liqueur, served blush-pink and ice-cold.

Vodka Sour 6 min Tart
Batch

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 oz citrus vodka
  • 3/4 oz orange liqueur
  • 3/4 oz lime juice
  • 1/2 oz cranberry juice

Smart swaps

orange liqueur: Orange curacao works in tiki drinks. Triple sec works in most citrus sours.

lime juice: Use lemon only in flexible sours. Keep lime for Margaritas, Daiquiris, Mojitos, and tiki drinks.

Method

  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.
  2. Fine-strain into a chilled coupe.
  3. Garnish with orange peel.
