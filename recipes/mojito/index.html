Long Rum Drink

Mojito

Mint, lime, white rum, sugar, and soda water, built tall and fragrant.

Long Rum Drink 7 min Fresh
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 oz white rum
  • 3/4 oz lime juice
  • 3/4 oz simple syrup
  • 8 mint leaves
  • Soda water

Smart swaps

white rum: Lightly aged rum works if it is not too sweet or dark.

lime juice: Use lemon only in flexible sours. Keep lime for Margaritas, Daiquiris, Mojitos, and tiki drinks.

simple syrup: Use honey syrup or agave syrup, but start slightly lighter because they read sweeter.

soda water: Sparkling water works as a direct swap.

Method

  1. Gently press mint with syrup and lime in a highball.
  2. Add rum, ice, and soda.
  3. Lift with a spoon and garnish with mint.
