Ginger Tea Collins

Black tea, ginger, lemon, honey, and bubbles, built like a Collins.

Zero Proof 6 min Spicy
Batch

Ingredients

  • 3 oz strong black tea
  • 3/4 oz lemon juice
  • 1/2 oz honey syrup
  • Ginger syrup or fresh ginger
  • Soda water

Smart swaps

lemon juice: Use lime for a sharper version in many sours, but expect the drink to taste brighter.

honey syrup: Use simple syrup for a cleaner drink or agave syrup for a softer roundness.

soda water: Sparkling water works as a direct swap.

Method

  1. Shake tea, lemon, honey, and ginger with ice.
  2. Strain into an iced Collins glass.
  3. Top with soda.
