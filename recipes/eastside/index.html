Cucumber Mint Sour

Eastside

Gin, lime, cucumber, mint, and simple syrup, cool as a linen shirt.

Cucumber Mint Sour 7 min Garden
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 oz gin
  • 3/4 oz lime juice
  • 3/4 oz simple syrup
  • 3 cucumber slices
  • 6 mint leaves

Smart swaps

lime juice: Use lemon only in flexible sours. Keep lime for Margaritas, Daiquiris, Mojitos, and tiki drinks.

simple syrup: Use honey syrup or agave syrup, but start slightly lighter because they read sweeter.

Method

  1. Muddle cucumber and mint lightly.
  2. Shake with gin, lime, syrup, and ice.
  3. Fine-strain into a coupe.
