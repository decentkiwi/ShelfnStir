Sparkling Classic

French 75

Gin, lemon, sugar, and sparkling wine. A dinner-party accelerant in a flute.

Sparkling Classic 6 min Celebratory
Batch

Ingredients

  • 1 oz gin
  • 1/2 oz lemon juice
  • 1/2 oz simple syrup
  • 3 oz sparkling wine

Smart swaps

lemon juice: Use lime for a sharper version in many sours, but expect the drink to taste brighter.

simple syrup: Use honey syrup or agave syrup, but start slightly lighter because they read sweeter.

sparkling wine: Prosecco works if it is dry and cold.

Method

  1. Shake gin, lemon, and syrup with ice.
  2. Strain into a flute.
  3. Top with sparkling wine and garnish with lemon peel.
