Rum Highball

Dark 'n Stormy

Dark rum over ginger beer with lime. Built fast, drunk slowly.

Rum Highball 4 min Spicy
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 oz dark rum
  • 1/2 oz lime juice
  • Ginger beer

Smart swaps

dark rum: Aged rum works when you want a lighter version.

lime juice: Use lemon only in flexible sours. Keep lime for Margaritas, Daiquiris, Mojitos, and tiki drinks.

Method

  1. Build lime and ginger beer over ice.
  2. Float dark rum on top.
  3. Garnish with lime.
