Garden Spritz

Cucumber, mint, lime, honey, and sparkling water for a dry, aromatic refresher.

Zero Proof 4 min Fresh
Batch

Ingredients

  • 3 cucumber slices
  • 6 mint leaves
  • 3/4 oz lime juice
  • 1/2 oz honey syrup
  • Sparkling water

Smart swaps

lime juice: Use lemon only in flexible sours. Keep lime for Margaritas, Daiquiris, Mojitos, and tiki drinks.

honey syrup: Use simple syrup for a cleaner drink or agave syrup for a softer roundness.

sparkling water: Soda water works as a direct swap.

Method

  1. Gently muddle cucumber and mint with lime and honey.
  2. Add ice and sparkling water.
  3. Lift with a spoon and garnish with mint.
