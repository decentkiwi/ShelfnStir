Herbal Stirred

Bijou

Gin, sweet vermouth, green Chartreuse, and orange bitters.

Herbal Stirred 5 min Jewel-toned
Batch

Ingredients

  • 1 oz gin
  • 1 oz sweet vermouth
  • 1 oz green Chartreuse
  • 1 dash orange bitters

Method

  1. Stir with ice until chilled.
  2. Strain into a coupe.
  3. Garnish with lemon or cherry.

Keep pouring
