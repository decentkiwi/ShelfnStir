Mai Tai

Aged rum, lime, orange curacao, and orgeat with almond depth.

Tiki Icon 7 min Layered
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 oz aged rum
  • 3/4 oz lime juice
  • 1/2 oz orange curacao
  • 1/2 oz orgeat

Smart swaps

lime juice: Use lemon only in flexible sours. Keep lime for Margaritas, Daiquiris, Mojitos, and tiki drinks.

orange liqueur: Orange curacao works in tiki drinks. Triple sec works in most citrus sours.

Method

  1. Shake with crushed ice.
  2. Pour unstrained into a rocks glass.
  3. Garnish with mint and lime.
