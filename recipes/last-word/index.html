Herbal Sour

Last Word

Gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino, and lime in equal parts.

Herbal Sour 5 min Herbal
Batch

Ingredients

  • 3/4 oz gin
  • 3/4 oz green Chartreuse
  • 3/4 oz maraschino liqueur
  • 3/4 oz lime juice

Smart swaps

lime juice: Use lemon only in flexible sours. Keep lime for Margaritas, Daiquiris, Mojitos, and tiki drinks.

Method

  1. Shake everything with ice.
  2. Fine-strain into a chilled coupe.
  3. Garnish with a cherry if desired.
