Modern Sour

Penicillin

Scotch, lemon, honey-ginger syrup, and a float of smoky Scotch.

Modern Sour 8 min Smoky
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 oz blended Scotch
  • 3/4 oz lemon juice
  • 3/4 oz honey ginger syrup
  • 1/4 oz smoky Scotch

Smart swaps

lemon juice: Use lime for a sharper version in many sours, but expect the drink to taste brighter.

honey syrup: Use simple syrup for a cleaner drink or agave syrup for a softer roundness.

Method

  1. Shake blended Scotch, lemon, and honey ginger syrup.
  2. Strain over ice.
  3. Float smoky Scotch on top.
