Equal Parts Mezcal

Naked and Famous

Mezcal, Aperol, yellow Chartreuse, and lime in equal parts.

Equal Parts Mezcal 5 min Smoky
Batch

Ingredients

  • 3/4 oz mezcal
  • 3/4 oz Aperol
  • 3/4 oz yellow Chartreuse
  • 3/4 oz lime juice

Smart swaps

lime juice: Use lemon only in flexible sours. Keep lime for Margaritas, Daiquiris, Mojitos, and tiki drinks.

Method

  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.
  2. Strain into a coupe.
  3. Serve ungarnished.
