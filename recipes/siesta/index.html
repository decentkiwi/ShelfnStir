Tequila Bitter Sour

Siesta

Tequila, Campari, grapefruit, lime, and simple syrup.

Tequila Bitter Sour 5 min Bittersweet
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 oz blanco tequila
  • 1/2 oz Campari
  • 1/2 oz grapefruit juice
  • 1/2 oz lime juice
  • 1/2 oz simple syrup

Smart swaps

lime juice: Use lemon only in flexible sours. Keep lime for Margaritas, Daiquiris, Mojitos, and tiki drinks.

simple syrup: Use honey syrup or agave syrup, but start slightly lighter because they read sweeter.

Method

  1. Shake all ingredients with ice.
  2. Strain into a coupe.
  3. Garnish with grapefruit.
