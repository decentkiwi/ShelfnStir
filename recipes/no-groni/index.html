No-Groni Spritz

Pomegranate, orange, tea, bitters-style notes, and soda for an aperitivo shape.

Zero Proof 5 min Bitter
Batch

Ingredients

  • 2 oz pomegranate juice
  • 1 oz orange juice
  • 2 oz strong black tea
  • Soda water
  • Orange slice

Smart swaps

soda water: Sparkling water works as a direct swap.

Method

  1. Build juices and tea over ice.
  2. Top with soda.
  3. Garnish with orange.
