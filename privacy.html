Privacy

What we collect, and what stays on your device

Without an account. Your pantry shelf and favorites are saved only in your browser or app (local storage on the website, on-device storage in the app). They are never sent to our servers. Star ratings use a random token saved on your device, not your identity, so they don't require an account either.

With an optional account. Creating an account is never required to browse recipes, use the pantry matcher, or rate a drink. If you do create one, we store your email address, display name, and a securely hashed password (we never store or transmit your actual password). Signing in lets your favorites and pantry shelf sync across your devices, and lets you leave comments, which show your display name publicly on the recipe page.

Where it's stored. Account and recipe data is stored in a Postgres database hosted by Neon, and served through Cloudflare's network. We don't share this data with advertisers or other third parties, and we don't run any analytics or advertising trackers.

Session cookie. Signing in sets one cookie used only to keep you signed in. It isn't used for tracking or advertising.

Deleting your account. You can permanently delete your account, including your favorites, pantry shelf, and comments, at any time from the Account screen. This can't be undone.

Age. Shelf&Stir is a cocktail recipe resource and isn't directed at children. If you're not of legal drinking age where you live, please stick to the zero-proof recipes.
