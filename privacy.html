Privacy Policy

What we collect, and what stays on your device

Last updated October 6, 2026

The short version

  • You can browse recipes, use the pantry matcher, and rate drinks without an account.
  • We don't run ads, analytics, or tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • If you create an account, we store your email, display name, and a salted hash of your password, plus the favorites, shelf, and comments you add.
  • You can permanently delete your account yourself at any time.

What we collect

Without an account. Your pantry shelf, favorites, and recently viewed drinks are saved only in your browser's local storage (or on-device storage in the app). They are never sent to us. When you rate a drink, your device generates a random token that is sent with the star rating so each device counts once. The token doesn't identify you.

With an account. Creating an account is optional. We store your email address, display name, and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself). Signing in syncs your favorites and shelf across devices and lets you comment. Comments are public and show your display name. Ratings you submit while signed in are linked to your account.

Technical data. Like any website, the services that deliver Shelf&Stir process your IP address and basic request details (such as the page requested and browser type) to serve the pages and protect against abuse. Our hosting providers may keep short-lived request logs. We use IP addresses only momentarily to rate-limit requests, and we don't store them in our database.

Cookies and local storage

When you sign in, we set one session cookie so you stay signed in. It is HttpOnly, lasts up to 30 days, and is used for nothing else. We also use your browser's local storage for your shelf, favorites, recent drinks, and rating token. These are strictly necessary for the features you use, so we don't show a cookie banner. Signing out or clearing your browser data removes them.

How we use your information

We use it only to run the service: to sign you in, sync your saved items, show your comments, count ratings, keep the service secure, and respond to requests. We don't use it for advertising or profiling.

Who handles your data

We use a small number of service providers to run Shelf&Stir. They process data on our behalf and only to provide their service:

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and API and delivers it over their network.
  • Neon hosts the database that stores accounts, favorites, shelves, ratings, and comments.
  • GitHub Pages may serve the static pages of the website.

Fonts and images are hosted by us. We don't load third-party scripts, analytics, advertising, or social media widgets.

How long we keep it

Account data stays until you delete your account. Local data stays on your device until you clear it. Our database provider may keep short-lived backups after a deletion before they are overwritten.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account in the app (Account, then Delete account) or on the website (Delete account in the header while signed in). This removes your account, favorites, shelf, and comments. Star ratings you gave remain as anonymous totals, with the link to your account removed. Deletion can't be undone.

Security

Traffic is encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Sign-in and write requests are rate-limited, and session cookies are HttpOnly. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, or to object to how it's used. Deleting your account covers deletion. For any other request, contact us using the details below and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

Age

Shelf&Stir is a cocktail recipe resource and isn't directed at children. You must be of legal drinking age where you live, and at least 18, to create an account. If you're not of legal drinking age, please stick to the zero-proof recipes. If we learn that an account belongs to someone who isn't eligible, we'll delete it.

International use

Our providers operate globally, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the date above, and we'll make the change clear before it takes effect if it's significant.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: open an issue at github.com/decentkiwi/ShelfnStir/issues. Please don't post passwords or other personal details publicly. Say that you have a privacy request and we'll arrange a private way to follow up.

See also our Terms of Use.
