Privacy

Your shelf stays on your device

Shelf&Stir saves selected ingredients in your browser so the matcher can remember your home shelf between visits.

Those selections are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to a server by this static version of the site.

The site does not include accounts, payments, comments, or recipe submissions. If those features are added later, the privacy policy should be updated before launch.

You can clear your saved shelf from the matcher by using the Clear all button.
