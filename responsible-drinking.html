Responsible drinking

Make good drinks and good decisions

Shelf&Stir is intended for adults of legal drinking age.

Please enjoy cocktails responsibly, pace your drinks, eat food, drink water, and never drive after drinking.

Recipes are provided for informational and entertainment purposes. Adjust serving sizes thoughtfully and avoid serving alcohol to anyone who should not drink.

The zero-proof recipes are included for nights when you want the ritual of a cocktail without alcohol.
