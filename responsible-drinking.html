Responsible drinking

Make good drinks and good decisions

Shelf&Stir is intended for adults of legal drinking age. Recipes are provided for information and entertainment, so please enjoy them thoughtfully.

At the bar

  • Pace yourself, eat food, and drink water between cocktails.
  • Never drive or operate machinery after drinking. Plan a ride before you start.
  • Remember that one cocktail can hold more than one standard drink, especially spirit-forward ones like a Martini or Negroni.
  • Never serve alcohol to anyone under the legal drinking age, or to anyone who shouldn't drink.

Health

  • Alcohol isn't recommended during pregnancy or when breastfeeding.
  • Alcohol can interact with some medications and health conditions. Ask a doctor or pharmacist if you're unsure.
  • If alcohol is causing problems for you or someone you know, talk to a doctor or contact a local support service. You're not alone.

Eggs and allergens

  • Some drinks (like the Clover Club, Ramos Gin Fizz, and Whiskey Sour) use raw egg white. Raw or undercooked eggs may carry a risk of foodborne illness. Use fresh, pasteurized eggs, and skip them if you're pregnant, elderly, very young, or immunocompromised.
  • Recipes may include dairy, nuts (such as the almond orgeat in a Mai Tai), or other allergens. Always check each ingredient.

Zero-proof options

Our zero-proof recipes are here for nights when you want the ritual of a cocktail without the alcohol. They're a great choice for anyone who isn't drinking, and for guests who would rather not.
